Before you send anything

  • Check against your current customers. Check none of these already buy from you through an Australian wholesaler. Power Farming's dealers also stock Kea trailers, so expect them to compare.
  • Freight times. Sea freight from India to Brisbane is usually slower than from China, so the emails say "dependable lead times" rather than "faster freight". Once you've confirmed your order-to-yard time, we can make that line specific.
  • Sourcing labels. "Stated" means the dealer says it on their website. "Inference" is our read and should be confirmed on the call. Tier 3 builders get a capacity pitch, never the China angle.
  • Model specs in the PDFs are indicative. Check them against your real range before sending, and add your phone number and name to your Gmail signature.
