# Messaging and Scripts

Rules for every message: keep it short, make it specific to them, include one proof point, and ask for one thing. Write like a tradesperson, not a marketer. Personalise the first line from something real (their range, their location, a review, a post).

## Email sequence for new dealers (4 touches over ~3 weeks)

**Touch 1, Day 0: Introduction**

> **Subject:** Second trailer supplier for [Company]?
>
> Hi [First name],
>
> I saw you carry [their range, e.g. cage and tandem box trailers] out of [town]. I run Innovatix. We build ADR-compliant trailers in our own steel fabrication factory in India and ship them flat-packed to dealers in QLD and VIC.
>
> Dealers switch to us for three reasons: certified steel and welds they can check (we send the mill certs), a landed cost below Chinese imports, and terms where you pay when you sell.
>
> Would you be open to a 15-minute call, or me dropping by with a sample piece next time I'm in [region]?
>
> [Name] | Innovatix | [mobile]

**Touch 2, Day 3: Proof (reply to the same thread)**

> Hi [First name], here are a few photos of the weld and galvanising finish on our [7x4 cage] alongside the mill certificate for the steel. One of our [QLD] wholesalers has taken [X] containers since [year]. Happy to put you in touch with them.
>
> Worth a quick chat?

**Touch 3, Day 8: Phone call** (script below), then a short email: "Tried to call you today about…"

**Touch 4, Day 15: The offer**

> Hi [First name], one last note from me. We're offering a small number of dealers in [state] a **trial container**: a mixed load of our best sellers that you choose, on pay-when-sold terms. If it doesn't sell, you haven't tied up cash.
>
> If now's not the right time, just tell me when to check back.

No reply after that: move to `nurture` and contact again in 60-90 days with news (a new model, an arrival date, a case study).

## Cold call script (dealer)

**Opener (10 seconds):**
"Hi [name], it's [your name] from Innovatix. We make trailers in our own factory in India and supply dealers in Queensland and Victoria. Have I caught you at an OK time for two minutes?"

**Reason for the call:**
"I'm calling dealers in [region] because a lot of them tell us they're fed up with the steel and weld quality on imported trailers. Is that something you're seeing?"

→ **Yes:** "What sort of problems? … How are you handling warranty on those?" (Let them talk. Note every pain point.)
→ **No/happy:** "Good to hear. Who are you buying from now? … Most of our dealers still use their existing supplier. They use us as a second source for [models] at a better landed cost. Would a second source be useful if your current supply got delayed?"

**Qualify:** volumes, top models, current source, who decides (see 02-target-segments).

**Close for the next step:**
"Best next step is for me to come and show you a sample. I'll bring the mill certs and a cut section of the weld so you can see it for yourself. I'm in [region] on [date]. Does [day/time] work?"

**If they say "send me something":** "Sure. What would make it worth your time: pricing on your top 3 models, or the compliance and steel docs?" Send it the same day, and book the follow-up call before hanging up.

## Visit agenda (45 minutes)

1. Their business: what sells, what's hurting (10 minutes)
2. Who we are: factory photos and video on a tablet (5 minutes)
3. **Hands-on proof:** a weld cut section, gal thickness (bring a coating gauge and let them measure), mill certs (10 minutes)
4. Their top 3-5 models priced against what they pay now (10 minutes)
5. The Trial Container Program: walk through the terms (5 minutes)
6. Agree the next step and date (5 minutes)

Afterwards: tell the bot what happened ("Visited X, spoke to Y, they want…") and it logs the visit and sets the follow-up.

## LinkedIn

**Connection note (≤300 characters):**
"Hi [name], I run Innovatix. We build ADR-compliant trailers in our own steel fab factory and supply dealers across QLD and VIC. I'd love to connect with people in the [trailer/hire/rural] space in [state]."

**After they accept:**
"Thanks for connecting, [name]. Quick question: are you sourcing any trailers from overseas at the moment? We're helping dealers add a second source with better steel and pay-when-sold terms. Happy to send through specs if useful."

**Posting (1-2 a week):** weld close-ups, galvanising, containers being loaded, assembly time-lapses, customer yards full of our trailers, mill certs (with sensitive details blurred). Proof beats slogans.

## Segment-specific angles

| Segment | Opening angle |
|---|---|
| Trailer manufacturer | "Add capacity without hiring welders: chassis, kits or complete trailers built to your drawings, under your brand." |
| Hire company | "Fleet trailers with heavier gal and certified welds: fewer repairs between hires, a lower cost per hire." |
| Rural/ag | "Farm trailers built heavy, galvanised properly, priced under the imports." |
| Caravan/camper/boat | "Off-road chassis and boat trailers from certified steel, white-label available." |
| Auto/4x4 accessory | "Add trailers to your range with no stock risk: pay when you sell." |
| Fleet/government | "ADR-compliant, VIN-plated trailers at fleet pricing, supplied through [local partner dealer]." |
