# New Zealand Market

New Zealand is a smaller market than Australia (about 5 million people), but trailers are everywhere. Farms, tradies, lifestyle blocks and boaties all tow, and a handful of national buyers control a large share of volume. The aim is **3-4 containers a month from 3-5 accounts**, mostly high-volume buyers rather than many small dealers.

Prospect list, brochures and emails: `research/nz/` (hit list page and PDFs).

## Who buys in volume

| Channel | Examples | Why they matter |
|---|---|---|
| **Importers that assemble locally** | Lucca Trailers (parts made in China, assembled in NZ), Gorilla Trailers / Half Price Imports | Already buy flat-packs from overseas, so switching suppliers is easy. **Highest priority.** |
| **High-volume online sellers** | Trade Me sellers (Tuff Trailers, Best Trailers, X-Trailers), Trade Tested | Sell hundreds of trailers a year on price and reviews |
| **Hardware retail** | Mitre 10 (Jobmate trailers) | The biggest single volume opportunity (private label). Long sales cycle. |
| **Hire fleets** | Hirepool, Hireace, Kennards, TR Group | Replace fleets on a cycle. Durability sells. |
| **Rural distributors and co-ops** | Power Farming (21 dealerships), Farmlands, PGG Wrightson | One agreement reaches every farming region |
| **NZ builders** | Kea Trailers, Prescott, TrailerWorx | Component or capacity partner only. Never pitch against their NZ-made brand. |

## What's different from Australia

- **Compliance:** New Zealand doesn't run on ADRs. Trailers must pass **NZ entry certification** and a **Warrant of Fitness (WoF)**. The buyer's certifier signs them off and they get an NZ VIN. Our ADR-approved drawbars, CTA-approved couplings and build documentation help that process, but don't replace it. **TO CONFIRM:** check the exact NZ requirements for our models with an NZ entry certifier or Waka Kotahi (NZTA) before the first shipment, including brake rules by weight.
- **Duty:** the **NZ–India free trade agreement** was concluded in December 2025, and NZ removes duty on all Indian goods once it's in force. (An Indian government document indicates it was signed in April 2026; entry into force not confirmed at the time of research.) Use it as a sales point, worded as "once it comes into force", until it's confirmed.
- **Popular sizes:** 7x4 and 8x4 box and cage trailers dominate domestic sales. Farm buyers want quad, stock-crate and tandem trailers.
- **Ports:** Auckland, Tauranga and Lyttelton (Christchurch). Price landed in the buyer's nearest port, in NZD.
- **Strong "NZ made" sentiment** among some builders (Trailer World and Reid market against imports). Leave them alone. The import-friendly buyers are the targets.

## Pitch (one line)

> Certified-steel trailers from our own factory, already selling to Australian dealers, landed in New Zealand for less than Chinese imports, with ADR-approved drawbars, CTA-approved couplings, VINs and NZ certification documents, mixed containers and pay-as-you-sell terms.

## Events

| Event | Where | Typical month | Why go |
|---|---|---|---|
| **National Fieldays** | Mystery Creek, Hamilton | June | The Southern Hemisphere's biggest agricultural show. Every rural dealer and trailer maker attends. |
| Central Districts Field Days | Feilding | March | Lower North Island farm buyers |
| South Island Agricultural Field Days | Kirwee, Canterbury | March (usually every 2 years) | South Island farm buyers |
| Auckland boat shows | Auckland | Varies | Boat trailer buyers |

Dates and frequency change, so check each event's website before booking.

## 90-day plan

1. **Weeks 1-2:** confirm NZ certification requirements with an entry certifier. Prepare NZD landed pricing for Auckland, Tauranga and Lyttelton.
2. **Weeks 2-4:** email and call the Tier 1 importers (Lucca, Gorilla, Trade Tested) and Power Farming. Book video calls.
3. **Weeks 4-8:** plan an NZ trip (Auckland and Hamilton, then Christchurch) once 6+ meetings are booked. Send a sample unit to the strongest lead.
4. **Weeks 8-12:** first trial container. Start the Mitre 10 and Hirepool conversations in parallel, since both take longer.
