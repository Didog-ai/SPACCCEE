# Trade Shows and Events

**Strategy:** In year 1, **walk** shows rather than exhibit. Collect exhibitor lists before you go (the bot can research and load them as prospects). Book 5-10 short meetings at stands in advance. Bring a cut weld sample and the evidence pack. Exhibit only at the 1-2 shows where buyers clearly gather.

> Dates and frequency change. Always check the official website before booking. The months below are typical, not guaranteed. Ask the bot to check current dates ("check the dates for AgQuip this year").

## Agricultural field days (rural/ag buyers, farm trailer dealers, machinery dealers)

| Event | Where | Typical month |
|---|---|---|
| Wimmera Machinery Field Days | Longerenong, VIC | March |
| Farmfest | Darling Downs (Toowoomba region), QLD | June |
| AgQuip | Gunnedah, NSW | August |
| Dowerin Machinery Field Days | Dowerin, WA | August |
| Henty Machinery Field Days | Henty, NSW | September |
| Elmore Field Days | Elmore, VIC | October |
| Yorke Peninsula Field Days | Paskeville, SA | September (usually every 2 years) |

## Caravan, camping, 4x4 and boating (off-road, camper and boat trailer dealers)

- State **caravan and camping shows** in Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane, Adelaide and Perth (held throughout the year; check each one's dates)
- **Boat shows** (e.g. Sydney, Sanctuary Cove, Melbourne): boat trailer dealers and builders
- 4x4/outdoor expos

## Commercial, trade and hire

- **Brisbane Truck Show** (May, every 2 years): the trailer/transport industry
- **Hire & Rental Industry Association (HRIA)** conference/expo: hire fleet buyers
- **Civil, construction and plant expos** (e.g. Diesel Dirt & Turf Expo): plant trailer buyers
- Local government expos and conferences: council fleet managers

## Industry associations (member lists = prospect lists)

- Commercial Vehicle Industry Association of Australia (CVIAA): trailer manufacturers
- Caravan Industry Association of Australia and the state associations
- Hire & Rental Industry Association (HRIA)
- Tractor and Machinery Association (TMA): machinery dealers
- Australia India Business Council (AIBC): networking and credibility for an Indian manufacturer

## Show checklist

**Before (2-3 weeks):**
- [ ] Get the exhibitor list and have the bot research and score it (`hunt` or chat)
- [ ] Email/call the top 15 to book a 10-minute stand visit
- [ ] Print 50 evidence packs and order business cards
- [ ] Pack the weld cut section, a gal sample and a coating gauge
- [ ] Load the tablet with the factory video and photo gallery

**On the day:**
- [ ] Note every conversation straight away (voice memo is fine)
- [ ] Photograph their business cards

**Within 48 hours:**
- [ ] Tell the bot about every conversation. It logs each one, sets the stage, and drafts the follow-ups.
- [ ] Send the follow-ups the same week
