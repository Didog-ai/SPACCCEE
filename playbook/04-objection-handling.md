# Objection Handling

Pattern: **acknowledge, then ask, then answer with proof, then propose a small next step.** Never argue.

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| **"Indian-made? Never heard of Indian trailers."** | "Fair. Most people haven't. That's why we lead with documents, not promises. Here's the mill certificate for the steel, the welders' certifications and the gal spec. Want to put a coating gauge on it yourself?" Then offer a sample unit or a reference call with an existing wholesaler. |
| **"We're happy with our current supplier."** | "Good. I'm not asking you to drop them. Most of our dealers use us as a **second source**. What happens to your yard if their next shipment is late or a batch has problems?" |
| **"Chinese is cheaper."** | "What are you paying landed, per unit, for your [7x4 cage]?" Compare on the same basis (landed, compliance, warranty claims, assembly time). Our landed price is lower. If it's close on a model, the steel quality and pay-when-sold terms decide it. |
| **"Quality of imports is bad. Why would yours be different?"** | "Because we own the factory and the steel is certified. You don't have to trust me: here are the certs, here's a cut weld, and here's a customer you can ring." |
| **"Compliance and registration worries."** | "Every trailer is built to the ADRs and carries a VIN. Here's the compliance documentation you get with each unit." (Have the actual documents ready. See 00-company-facts, TO CONFIRM.) |
| **"Lead times are too long."** | "Order-to-yard is about [X] weeks (CONFIRM). Most dealers set up a rolling monthly container so there's always stock on the water. With pay-when-sold, holding more stock doesn't tie up your cash." |
| **"We don't have time or staff to assemble flat-packs."** | "Assembly takes about [X] hours per unit (CONFIRM), with a guide and video. We can also [train your team / connect you with a local assembler]. Built-up display units are available too." |
| **"Warranty: who fixes it?"** | Explain the warranty, parts held in Australia, and the claims process (CONFIRM details). Offer a written warranty in the first order. |
| **"Too much risk for a full container."** | "That's exactly why we offer the Trial Container: you choose the mix, and you pay when they sell. Or start with one assembled sample unit, credited against your first order." |
| **"Send me a price list."** | "Happy to. Which 3-5 models move most for you? I'll price those landed in [city] so it's apples with apples, and call you Thursday to go through it." |
| **"Not now."** | "When do you normally restock or review suppliers?" Set `nurture` with a dated next action, and send something useful in the meantime. |
| **"How do I know you'll be around in a year?"** | Talk about the factory (photos/video, offer a factory visit), years in business, current customers since [year], parts and support in Australia. |
