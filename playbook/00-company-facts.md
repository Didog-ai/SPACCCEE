# Innovatix: Company Facts

The bot treats this file as the truth about Innovatix. Keep it current. Anything marked **TO CONFIRM** must be asked of the owner, not assumed or quoted to a customer.

## Confirmed

| Topic | Fact |
|---|---|
| What we are | Steel fabrication business with our own factory in India. We manufacture trailers and export them to Australia. |
| Product range | All trailer types: box, tandem, cage/tradie, car carrier, plant/equipment, flatbed, tipper, off-road/camper, boat, custom builds. Nothing is ruled out. |
| How it ships | Flat-packed in shipping containers. Each container carries the trailers plus all loose parts for assembly in Australia. |
| Mixed loads | Yes. One container can hold a mix of models. |
| Compliance | Trailers are built to Australian Design Rules (ADRs) and carry VINs. |
| Price | Landed price per trailer is well below comparable Chinese imports. |
| Proof available | Steel grade documentation, mill certificates, welder certifications, galvanising specs, photos, test reports, and testimonials from current customers. (No ISO certificate, and we don't need one to sell.) |
| Trials | We can send sample units, or one container on trial terms. |
| Credit terms | We offer credit: wholesalers can pay when they sell the trailers. |
| Capacity | Up to **20 containers/month** now. Current volume is about **4/month**, so we have plenty of room to grow. |
| Current customers | 4-5 wholesalers in **QLD** and **VIC (Melbourne)**. No exclusive territories. |
| Why they buy | Price, quality and lead time. Many are tired of the steel quality on Chinese trailers and want a second source because of tariff and supply risk. |
| Sales team | The owner. They visit dealers and attend shows in person. |

## TO CONFIRM (the bot must ask, not guess)

- **Lead time.** Weeks from order to factory dispatch, plus sea freight to Brisbane/Melbourne/Sydney/Fremantle. Measure it on the next 3 orders.
- **Price list.** Landed AUD price per model, ex-port or delivered, by container quantity.
- **Container capacity.** How many trailers of each model fit in a 40ft HC container.
- **Typical gross margin per container.** Needed to set the marketing budget and credit limits.
- **Compliance paperwork.** What each buyer receives: ADR compliance documents, RAV (Register of Approved Vehicles) entry or approval numbers, VIN plates, assembly instructions. Who is the legal "manufacturer/importer" on record?
- **Steel specs.** Grades (for example equivalent to AS/NZS 3679 / 1163), galvanising standard and coating thickness (hot-dip to AS/NZS 4680?), axle, coupling, brake and light suppliers.
- **Warranty.** Length, what's covered, and who handles claims in Australia.
- **Parts and support in Australia.** Spare parts stock and who handles warranty repairs.
- **Assembly.** Hours per trailer to assemble. Can we supply an assembly guide or video, or train the dealer's team?
- **Branding.** Can we build under the buyer's brand (white label)? Minimum order for custom builds?
- **Credit terms in practice.** Maximum days before payment is due regardless of sales, credit limit per customer, and whether stock is registered on the PPSR (see 07-credit-and-risk).
- **Testimonials.** Which current customers will take a reference call?
