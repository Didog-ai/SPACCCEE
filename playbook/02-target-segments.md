# Target Segments and the Ideal Customer Profile

## Ideal customer profile (score 1-10)

A prospect scores high when it:
- **Sells or uses trailers in volume:** 50+ trailers a year, or a fleet of 30+ (+3)
- **Already imports, or buys from someone who does:** they understand container lead times and flat-pack assembly (+2)
- **Has a yard and staff who can assemble flat-packs** (+1)
- **Has had quality or supply problems with current product** (forum posts, reviews, what they tell you) (+2)
- **Is in a state or region where we have no current customer** (+1)
- **Is an owner-run business that can decide quickly** (+1)

Scores of 7+ get contacted this week. 4-6 go into the sequence. Below 4: `nurture` or `not_a_fit`.

## Segments

### 1. Trailer dealers and wholesalers (`trailer_dealer`): PRIORITY
- **Who:** Trailer sales yards, trailer wholesalers that supply smaller retailers, and towbar/trailer shops that sell trailers.
- **Find them:** Google Maps ("trailer sales" + each regional town), trailer listing sites, Gumtree/Facebook Marketplace (dealers listing many units), Yellow Pages, and visible signage at shows.
- **Volume:** 0.5-3 containers/month.
- **Pitch:** Margin, quality and pay-when-sold. "Test us with one mixed container."
- **Target order:** NSW (Sydney, Newcastle, Central Coast, Wollongong, Hunter, Central West, New England), WA (Perth metro, South West), SA (Adelaide), then regional QLD/VIC, then TAS/NT.

### 2. Trailer manufacturers (`trailer_manufacturer`)
- **Who:** Australian trailer builders, from small workshops to large brands.
- **Why they'd buy:** Labour shortages and steel costs. They can buy **chassis, kits, or complete white-label trailers** and spend their own labour on high-margin custom work.
- **Pitch:** "Add capacity without adding welders." Offer to build to their drawings under their brand.
- **Sensitivity:** Some will see us as a threat. Approach them as a supplier/partner, not a competitor.

### 3. Hire companies (`hire_company`)
- **Who:** National and regional equipment-hire chains, trailer-hire businesses, servo/hardware trailer-hire networks, and moving/truck-hire companies.
- **Why:** Fleets of standard box/cage trailers that get replaced on a cycle. Durability and galvanising quality matter a lot.
- **Pitch:** Total cost of ownership: heavier gal, stronger welds, fewer repairs, and a fleet price.
- **Find them:** Hire & Rental Industry Association (HRIA) member lists, Google "trailer hire" by city.

### 4. Rural and agricultural (`rural_ag`)
- **Who:** Farm machinery dealers, rural merchandise chains and independent rural stores, livestock and produce businesses.
- **Products:** Farm/stock crates, tippers, plant trailers, heavy-duty box trailers.
- **Find them:** Field day exhibitor lists (05-trade-shows-and-events), dealer locators on machinery brand websites.

### 5. Caravan, camper, off-road and boat (`caravan_camper_boat`)
- **Who:** Camper-trailer and off-road trailer dealers, caravan dealers adding camper trailers, and boat dealers (boat trailers).
- **Pitch:** Off-road chassis quality, the gal spec, and private-label camper chassis or tubs.
- **Find them:** Caravan & Camping show exhibitor lists, boat show exhibitor lists, Caravan Industry Association member lists.

### 6. Fleet buyers (`fleet_buyer`)
- **Who:** Councils, utilities (power and water), mining services, civil construction, landscaping and tree services, plumbers/electricians with fleets, event companies.
- **Route:** Usually through tenders, panels or a local dealer. See 06-government-and-fleet.

### 7. Government (`government`)
- Federal, state and local government purchasing through tenders and pre-approved supplier panels. See 06-government-and-fleet.

### 8. Auto and 4x4 accessory chains (`auto_accessory`)
- **Who:** Towbar fitters, 4x4 accessory stores, ute canopy/tray businesses. They already sell to trailer buyers.
- **Pitch:** Add trailers to the range with no stock risk (consignment).

### 9. Online resellers (`online_reseller`)
- **Who:** Businesses selling trailers through their own websites or marketplaces at volume.
- **Pitch:** Price and pay-when-sold. Check they have a yard for assembly and pickup.

## Qualifying questions (ask on the first real call)

1. How many trailers do you sell (or run) a year? Which types are your top sellers?
2. Where do you buy them now? Local build, import, or both?
3. What's your biggest headache with your current supply: quality, warranty claims, lead time, price, or availability?
4. Have you imported in containers before? Who assembles?
5. Who decides on a new supplier, and what would they need to see?
6. What would a first order look like for you?
7. Timing: when do you next restock?
