# Credit Terms and Risk (Pay-When-Sold)

Pay-when-sold credit is our strongest closing tool, and our biggest financial risk. At 10 containers a month, much of our working capital will sit in dealer yards. Protect it.

> This is general guidance, not legal or financial advice. Have an Australian commercial lawyer draft the supply/consignment agreement and confirm the PPSR setup.

## Rules before extending credit

1. **Written supply agreement** for every customer. It covers retention of title, the payment trigger (on sale or by a hard deadline), stock reporting, insurance, and return of unsold stock.
2. **Retention of title + PPSR registration.** In Australia, goods supplied on consignment or retention-of-title terms should be registered on the **Personal Property Securities Register (PPSR)**. Without registration we can lose the stock if a dealer goes into administration.
3. **Hard maximum term.** E.g. 120 days. After that, unsold units are invoiced regardless.
4. **Credit limit per customer.** Start new customers at **1 container** of exposure. Raise it only after on-time payment history.
5. **Monthly stock report** from each dealer (units sold and on hand). Spot-check yards on visits. VIN-level tracking.
6. **Credit check** before the first container: ABN lookup, years trading, director checks, trade references, and a credit report from an Australian bureau.
7. **Insurance.** The dealer insures consigned stock (our interest noted). Consider export credit insurance, e.g. through ECGC (India).
8. **Exposure cap overall.** Set a maximum total outstanding across all dealers, linked to what the business can survive losing.

## Warning signs (the bot should flag these)

- Payments slipping past the agreed date
- Stock reports late or vague
- Asking for more stock while old stock is unpaid
- Sudden changes in staff, ownership or premises
- Discounting our trailers heavily (a sign of cash pressure)

## Alternatives for higher-risk buyers

- A deposit (e.g. 20-30%) with the balance on sale
- Payment against shipping documents for repeat buyers once trust is established
- Trial container only, no second container until the first is paid
