# Strategy: 4 to 10 Containers a Month

## 1. The numbers

We need **+6 containers/month**. They come from two places:

| Source | Target | How |
|---|---|---|
| Existing 4-5 wholesalers | **+2/month** (4 → 6) | Widen the range they take from us (more models per container), mixed loads, a display-stock program, and quarterly business reviews. Existing customers are the cheapest growth. |
| New accounts | **+4/month** | About **4-5 new regular accounts** averaging ~1 container/month each. Some will be bigger. |

**Funnel to land 4-5 new regular accounts** (assumed conversion rates; replace them with our own numbers after 90 days):

| Stage | Conversion | Number needed |
|---|---|---|
| Prospects researched and qualified | | **~220** |
| Contacted (email + call + LinkedIn) | 100% | 220 |
| Engaged (real conversation) | ~30% | ~65 |
| Meeting / site visit | ~50% | ~32 |
| Trial container or sample unit offered | ~40% | ~13 |
| Trial order placed | ~65% | ~8-9 |
| Become regular (reorder within 90 days) | ~55% | **~4-5** |

**Weekly activity this implies over ~6 months** (one person):
- 10-12 new prospects researched and added (the bot does most of this)
- 25-30 outreach touches (emails, calls, LinkedIn)
- 2-3 meetings or site visits
- 1 trial offer every two weeks

**Realistic timeline:** Month 1-2 builds the pipeline. Months 2-4 bring the first trial containers (shipping lead time sits inside this). Months 4-6 bring reorders. **10/month is a 6-9 month target, not 90 days.** Lead time is the biggest variable, so measure it.

## 2. Positioning: why Innovatix

The one-line pitch:

> **Australian-compliant trailers built from certified steel in our own Indian factory, landed for less than Chinese imports, with no money up front: you pay when you sell.**

Pillars, in the order buyers care about:

1. **Steel and weld quality you can verify.** Mill certificates, welder certs, galvanising specs and photos. Show, don't tell. Bring a weld/gal sample piece to every visit.
2. **Landed cost lower than China.** Better margin for the dealer at the same shelf price, or a sharper price at the same margin.
3. **Low risk to switch.** Pay-when-sold credit, a trial container, mixed loads, sample units. The dealer can test us without betting the business.
4. **A second source.** Supply diversification away from a single-country dependency. Dealers who rely 100% on China carry concentration risk on tariffs, anti-dumping actions, shipping disruption and quality.
5. **A factory, not a trader.** We make it ourselves, so we can customise, white-label and fix problems at the source.
6. **ADR compliant with VINs.** Ready to register.

> **Careful with tariff claims.** Under the India-Australia ECTA, most Indian goods enter Australia duty-free, and Chinese goods often do too under ChAFTA. Don't claim a tariff advantage over China unless a customs broker confirms it for our HS codes. Lead with *supply diversification* and *quality* instead. Confirm before using.

## 3. The offer that removes risk: "Trial Container Program"

A standard, repeatable first deal that every new buyer is offered:

- **1 mixed container** chosen from our 6-8 fastest sellers (the dealer picks the mix).
- **Pay-when-sold credit** with guardrails: a hard maximum term (e.g. 120 days) after which unsold stock is paid for or returned at the dealer's cost. Stock stays ours until paid and is registered on the PPSR. See 07-credit-and-risk.
- **Launch kit:** spec sheets, compliance docs, a photo pack for their website and marketplace listings, an assembly guide, and a point-of-sale banner.
- **Quality guarantee:** a written warranty plus replacement parts held in Australia.
- **Review at 60 days:** sell-through, issues, and the reorder plan.

For cautious buyers: **one assembled sample unit** delivered for inspection first, credited against their first container.

## 4. Growing existing accounts (+2/month)

- **Quarterly business review** with each wholesaler: what's selling, what they still buy from China, and why. Every model they still source elsewhere is a gap we can fill.
- **Range extension:** offer the models they don't take from us yet, in a mixed load.
- **Display stock program:** 1-2 built-up display units per yard, on credit.
- **Referral reward:** a credit on their next container for every introduction that becomes a new account outside their trading area.
- **Case studies:** turn each happy wholesaler into a testimonial (sell-through, quality, margin) for the new-business pitch.

## 5. Where new accounts come from (priority order)

1. **Trailer dealers and wholesalers in NSW, WA and SA.** New states with no conflict with current customers, and the same buying behaviour as today's customers. **Highest priority.**
2. **More dealers in QLD and VIC** in regional areas away from current customers.
3. **Australian trailer manufacturers.** Sell them flat-pack kits, chassis, or white-label complete trailers so they can expand their range without expanding their workshop.
4. **Hire companies.** Fleet volumes, repeat replacement cycles.
5. **Rural and ag dealers.** Farm trailers through machinery dealers and rural merchandise stores.
6. **Caravan, camper, off-road and boat dealers.** Specialist off-road/camper trailers and boat trailers.
7. **Fleet buyers and government.** Councils, utilities, mining, construction, landscaping. Win them through tenders and panels, usually with a local dealer partner. See 06-government-and-fleet.
8. **Online resellers.** Businesses selling trailers on marketplaces or their own e-commerce site.

Details and how to find each: 02-target-segments.

## 6. Channels

| Channel | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **In-person visits** | Highest close rate | Plan trips by region: 3-5 visits a day, every visit booked in advance. Bring samples and the evidence pack. |
| **Phone** | Qualify and book visits | Call the owner or buyer directly. Dealers answer phones. |
| **Email** | Introduction and follow-up | Short, specific, with photos. Use a 4-touch sequence (03-messaging-and-scripts). |
| **LinkedIn** | Credibility and reach | Owner profile and a company page with factory photos and videos. Connect with dealer principals. |
| **Trade shows and field days** | Meet many buyers at once | Walk shows first (cheap); exhibit only where buyers concentrate. See 05-trade-shows-and-events. |
| **Website and landing page** | Proof | One page for dealers: why Innovatix, the evidence pack, the trial program, and a contact form. |
| **Referrals** | Warm intros | From existing wholesalers and suppliers (axle, coupling and galvanising suppliers know every builder). |

## 8. The 90-day launch plan

**Weeks 1-2: Foundations**
- Fill in the TO CONFIRM items in 00-company-facts (lead time, price list, container capacity, warranty).
- Build the **evidence pack**: a one-page spec sheet per model, mill certs, welder certs, gal specs, compliance docs, 3 testimonials, and a photo set.
- Set up a landing page and the LinkedIn company page.
- Load existing customers into the CRM as `active` with their real monthly volumes.
- Hold a quarterly business review with every existing wholesaler.
- Bot: research and load the first 100 prospects (NSW and regional QLD/VIC dealers first).

**Weeks 3-6: Outreach engine**
- 25-30 touches/week, using the email sequence plus phone.
- Plan the first interstate trip (Sydney and regional NSW) once 8-10 meetings are booked.
- Walk the next relevant field day or show.
- Offer the first 3-4 trial containers.

**Weeks 7-12: Convert and expand**
- Ship the first trial containers. Follow up on their sell-through weekly.
- Trips to WA (Perth) and SA (Adelaide).
- Start the manufacturer/white-label and hire-company tracks.
- Register on government tender portals; shortlist panels (06-government-and-fleet).
- Review conversion rates and update the funnel numbers above.

## 9. KPIs (the bot tracks these)

- Containers ordered per month (target 10) and the active-customer run rate
- New prospects added per week (10-12)
- Outreach touches per week (25-30)
- Meetings per week (2-3)
- Trial offers per month (2-3), trial → regular conversion rate
- Number of active customers (target 9-10) and containers per customer
- Average days from first contact to first order
- Credit exposure outstanding vs the limit
