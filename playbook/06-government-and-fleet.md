# Government, Council and Fleet Sales

Government buyers purchase through **open tenders**, **requests for quote (RFQs)**, and **supplier panels** (pre-approved suppliers the buyer can order from without a full tender). Councils buy trailers regularly: parks, roads, waste and events departments.

## Realistic approach

1. **Partner first.** Many buyers prefer or require a supplier with an Australian business presence, local warranty support and delivery. The fastest route is often to **supply a dealer who already holds the panel or bids on the tender**. Ask every dealer prospect: "Do you supply councils or government? Are you on any panels?"
2. **Register directly** on the portals below to see what's being bought, the specs and the volumes. That's useful market intelligence even before we bid.
3. **Bid on small RFQs** once we have local entity/ABN, insurance, warranty and support arrangements in place.

## Where tenders are published (register on each; it's free)

Panel and portal names change. Have the bot check the current ones before you register.

## What you'll typically need

- An Australian Business Number (ABN) and an Australian entity, or a local partner
- Public liability and product liability insurance (amounts specified in each tender)
- ADR compliance documentation and VINs for every unit
- A warranty and service arrangement in Australia
- Work health and safety, quality and environmental statements (ISO isn't always required, but documented processes help)
- References from similar supply contracts

## Fleet buyers (private)

Mining services, civil contractors, utilities, landscaping and tree services, and event hire companies.
- Find the **fleet or procurement manager** on LinkedIn.
- Pitch total cost of ownership: galvanising and weld quality mean fewer repairs.
- Offer fleet pricing on a set order (e.g. 20 trailers), delivered through a partner dealer if they need local service.
